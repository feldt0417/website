Questions

How long does activation take?

Your plan turns on automatically as soon as the blockchain confirms your payment. That's usually a few minutes, depending on the coin. The panel updates by itself, so you can leave it open.

Which coins can I pay with?

Payments go through NOWPayments, which accepts Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, USDT and many more. You choose the coin on the payment page.

Where do I find my key and the download?

Both are in the panel. Your key is listed under Your keys, and the Download button unlocks while your plan is active.

Someone gave me a key. How do I use it?

Log in to the panel, paste it into Redeem a key and press Redeem. The plan is added to your account straight away.

What happens if I buy again before my plan runs out?

The new time is added on top of what you have left, so you never lose days. Lifetime never expires.

I sent less than the full amount. What now?

Partial payments can't switch a plan on automatically. They're checked by hand instead, so send the exact amount shown on the payment page to avoid delays.
